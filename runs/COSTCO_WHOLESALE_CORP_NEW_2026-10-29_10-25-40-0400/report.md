## Overview

Costco runs membership warehouses and e-commerce sites. Its stated model is low prices, a limited selection, high sales volume, and rapid inventory turnover. [10-K text, lines 185 and 188](10-K_0000909832-26-000093.txt).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco identifies price, selection, location, convenience, and service as sources of retail competition. It names Walmart, Target, and BJ's among relevant competitors. [10-K text, line 247](10-K_0000909832-26-000093.txt). Fiscal-year total revenue increased 10.14%, and the latest reported quarter's total revenue increased 11.10% from the comparable quarter. These are different periods; the arithmetic and sources appear below. Future growth is uncertain. The company identifies labor costs, competition, and warehouse openings among risks to results. [10-K text, lines 281 and 326](10-K_0000909832-26-000093.txt).
